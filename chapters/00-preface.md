Under the Hood: Building and Debugging Production Data Platforms
Audience and Purpose

Audience: Platform engineers, senior data engineers, and MLOps engineers who have moved past tutorials and are now responsible for keeping production systems alive. They know how to use Spark. They know how to write a DAG. They do not know how to debug the kernel when it breaks.

Purpose: This book provides clarity and wisdom for engineers who have lost the path. It teaches the skill of going one layer deeper: reading the transaction log, understanding the protocol, fixing the kernel, and building the tooling that prevents the next outage. It is not a survey of tools. It is a guide to the mindset of ownership.
